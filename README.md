# dna-exercise
hosts "exercise" assignment

## Prompt
Using the provided [dataset](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1OPNrVKYAg39cloXlXbl51EadEnEvEXe5?usp=sharing), design and build an AI native data product that empowers both technical and non-technical employees across the business to self serve analytics, including how you'd handle the bad data, your modeling approach, and how an LLM-based interface would stay grounded in truth rather than hallucinating metrics. You will submit all work to your recruiter and present it to a panel of technical peers and executives.

### Where we’d like you to focus

Ensure data quality and trust. Profile the data, identify the issues, and implement a documented, defensible treatment for each. Explain how your pipeline would catch these issues going forward.

Build the model. Show your modeling approach, including the grain, joins, and structure of the tables, and explain why you made those choices. Be explicit about how the model supports both technical and non-technical use.
Ground the interface. Show how the LLM interface would draw on governed metric definitions rather than guessing. Include how you’d test and monitor its answers against known results.

Protect access. Explain how you’d handle sensitive fields, user-level data, and access controls in the model and in the AI interface.

You may use any format that makes your reasoning clear. 

### Submission and Presentation
Submit your work to your recruiter, Kelly - kellyp@scribd.com 

Present to a panel of technical peers and executives. Plan for a mix of cross-functional audiences, and be ready to explain your decisions to both technical and business listeners.

Format: The presentation is 45 minutes in total. Plan for 20–25 minutes of presenting, and we'll use the remaining time for questions and discussion.

Pacing: Please plan your content to fit within your presentation time. You don't need to cover every detail of your work, so focus on the decisions and insights that matter most. Everything you submit will be available to the panel.
