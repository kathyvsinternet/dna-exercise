Model

One row per real thing, then a small set of metrics

A business reader uses the four metric files. An analyst can go one level down, to the subscription fact, and see the same filters the metric applied. The full column list is data/model/schema.yml.

dim_business

3 rows, one per house. Creator, content, and subscription rows point here.

dim_user

800 people after the duplicate drop. Subscription and event rows point here. Names, emails, and age bands are restricted.

dim_creator and dim_content

40 creators and 300 titles. Only MakerHarbor content has a creator. PageGarden and GatherSquare titles stay unattributed.

fct_subscription and fct_engagement_event

843 subscriptions and 6,000 events. A person can subscribe to more than one house, so the subscription grain is the subscription, not the person. Events with no title stay on the event fact and are left out of a house’s content count.
